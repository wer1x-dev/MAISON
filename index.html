03 — The atelier

Cut by hand, in a room above the shop.

Six people, four sewing machines and a cutting table that has been in the family since 1994. We make everything here — from the first paper pattern to the last buttonhole.

Every piece is fitted on three different bodies before it goes into production, because a silhouette only works if it works on more than one person.

— Aigerim, founder
